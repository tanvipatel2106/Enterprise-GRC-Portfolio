## Executive Summary
This project outlines a comprehensive **Cybersecurity Program, Risk Assessment, and Business Continuity Plan (BCP)** designed for **Stellar Scope Emporium**, an online-only e-commerce enterprise specializing in fine telescopes, astrophotography equipment, and sky-mapping software[cite: 1, 4]. The framework is engineered to safeguard digital and physical infrastructure, protect customer Personally Identifiable Information (PII) and payment transactions, and ensure robust business continuity[cite: 1, 4].

---

## Program Goals & Objectives
The program is built upon the classic **CIA Triad**, alongside robust risk management and incident response frameworks[cite: 1]:
*   **Confidentiality:** Protect sensitive customer data (PII, credit card details) and corporate intellectual property[cite: 1].
*   **Integrity:** Ensure system logs, configurations, and data remain authentic and uncompromised[cite: 1].
*   **Availability:** Guarantee uninterrupted access to e-commerce operations for customers and remote staff[cite: 1].
*   **Proactive Defense:** Implement automated vulnerability scanning, continuous monitoring, and role-based access control (RBAC)[cite: 1].

---

## Framework & Compliance
The security architecture aligns with major national and international standards to ensure strict regulatory adherence:
*   **GDPR:** Safeguarding personal data and privacy rights for clientele[cite: 1].
*   **PCI-DSS:** Securing credit card processing and payment transactions[cite: 1].
*   **ISO/IEC 27001:** Establishing guidelines for information security management and asset controls[cite: 1].

## Core Components & Architecture

### 1. Leadership & Personnel Security
*   **Governance Structure:** Managed by the CISO (overall program accountability), IT Manager (technical setup), and Compliance Officer (regulatory alignment)[cite: 1].
*   **Background Checks & Screening:** Tiered background checks (criminal, credit, drug testing) and mandatory NDAs mapped across organizational roles (Admin, IT, General)[cite: 1].
*   **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC):** Principle of least privilege enforced across all enterprise tools and databases[cite: 1].

### 2. Risk Management & Budget Allocation
A structured annual budget targets critical security enhancements:
*   **Encryption Implementation ($50,000):** SSL/TLS for data in transit and AES for data at rest[cite: 1].
*   **Audit Processes ($30,000 annually):** Bi-annual third-party assessments and automated vulnerability scanning[cite: 1].
*   **Training Programs ($20,000 annually):** Phishing simulations, security awareness platforms (e.g., KnowBe4), and role-specific education[cite: 1].
*   **Incident Response Tools ($25,000):** SIEM integration and forensic toolkits[cite: 1].
*   **Physical Security ($15,000):** Badge readers, CCTV surveillance, and server room climate controls[cite: 1].

### 3. Patch & Configuration Management
*   **Patch Priority Triage:** Critical patches applied within **48 hours**; high-priority patches within **one week**[cite: 1].
*   **Workflow:** Automated identification $\rightarrow$ Impact analysis $\rightarrow$ Isolated staging environment testing $\rightarrow$ Production deployment $\rightarrow$ Verification scan[cite: 1].
